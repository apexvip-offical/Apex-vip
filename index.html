ℹ️ How to play and fair play

Important points

  • This is a free practice coin game only. No real money is involved, and coins cannot be exchanged for money.
  • For ages 18+ only.
  • Every result comes from the browser crypto random generator. No hidden setting controls the result.
  • Everything is saved on your own phone (you can take a backup in Settings).

Games, odds and return

  • Coin Flip: 50/50 chance, wins pay 1.96× (98% return).
  • Math Rush: skill game. Each correct answer raises the multiplier (1.2× to 20×). A wrong answer or running out of time is a loss.
  • Chicken Road (Hard): each step is 75% safe, 8 steps, cash out any time (97% return).
  • Lucky Lottery: Pick 5 numbers out of 35. 2 matches pay 3×, 3 matches 25×, 4 matches 500×, 5 matches win the jackpot (about 95% return).
  • Mines: 5×5 board, 1/3/5/10 mines. The more mines, the bigger the multiplier (97% return).
  • Dice: multiplier = 97 / chance. Lower chance means a higher multiplier (97% return).
  • Hi-Lo: next card higher or lower. Every guess is 97% return, an equal card loses.
  • Plinko: 8 rows, Low/Medium/High risk. All three return about 97%.
  • Crash: the multiplier rises, cash out before the crash (97% return).

Take care of yourself

  • Keep the break reminder on. You can change its timing in the lobby.
  • If playing starts to feel like a habit or causes stress, take a break and talk to someone you trust.
